DESIGN HANDOFF · TURN 1
Ladderboard
One read-only board page at two widths, its day-one state, and the four Slack messages that do all the writing. Board and Slack cards share one vocabulary: mono for numbers, status badges for state, colour only ever meaning shortcut / setback / power-up. Bags are private — the board never shows what anyone is holding.
1a
Board page — mobile, populated
375 × 812
Tiles land at ~33px. Numbers stay in the corner, type reads from fill plus one glyph, tokens sit on the tile edge and cap at two with a +n. No tile is ever two things at once — shortcut, setback and power-up tiles never share a cell.
1b
Board page — day one, empty
375 × 812
The board is fully drawn from minute one — setbacks, shortcuts and power-up tiles are the content on day one. Tokens wait in a dashed start rail; the feed states its own purpose instead of apologising.
1c
Board page — desktop
1180 wide
Same components, no separate desktop layout — the three regions wrap. Board and standings pair up, the feed takes the full width because it is the reason people come back.
